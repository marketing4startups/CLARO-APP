# 💪 Employee Guide: Understanding & Preventing Burnout

## Know the Warning Signs

### Am I Experiencing Burnout?

Burnout doesn't happen overnight. It develops gradually through a combination of factors. Check if you're experiencing multiple signs:

#### Physical Warning Signs
- [ ] Chronic fatigue that rest doesn't fix
- [ ] Frequent headaches or muscle tension
- [ ] Sleep problems (too much or too little)
- [ ] Getting sick more often
- [ ] Changes in appetite or weight
- [ ] Relying on caffeine, sugar, or alcohol to cope

#### Emotional Warning Signs
- [ ] Feeling cynical or detached from work
- [ ] Persistent irritability or mood swings
- [ ] Feeling hopeless or helpless
- [ ] Reduced patience with colleagues
- [ ] Anxiety or panic attacks
- [ ] Depression or persistent sadness

#### Behavioral Warning Signs
- [ ] Withdrawing from coworkers or friends
- [ ] Working longer hours with less productivity
- [ ] Missing deadlines or making more mistakes
- [ ] Calling in sick more frequently
- [ ] Avoiding meetings or social events
- [ ] Procrastinating on important tasks

#### Work Performance Warning Signs
- [ ] Difficulty concentrating or remembering
- [ ] Loss of enthusiasm for projects
- [ ] Struggling to make decisions
- [ ] Increased conflicts with team members
- [ ] Negative attitude toward work
- [ ] Questioning your career choice

**If you check 3+ boxes, consider taking action:**

1. ✅ Complete the burnout assessment
2. ✅ Talk to your manager
3. ✅ Reach out to HR or EAP
4. ✅ Engage in wellness activities
5. ✅ Seek professional support if needed

## Prevention Strategies You Can Use

### 1. Manage Your Workload

**Actions You Can Take:**
- [ ] Regularly assess your workload capacity
- [ ] Say "no" to tasks that overextend you
- [ ] Break large projects into manageable pieces
- [ ] Set realistic deadlines
- [ ] Prioritize important tasks
- [ ] Ask for help when needed
- [ ] Communicate capacity limits to your manager

**Conversation Starter:**
"I want to do quality work. Can we discuss priorities and realistic timelines?"

### 2. Create Healthy Boundaries

**Actions You Can Take:**
- [ ] Set specific work hours and stick to them
- [ ] Don't check email after hours (unless truly needed)
- [ ] Take breaks throughout the day
- [ ] Use your vacation days
- [ ] Create a transition ritual between work and home
- [ ] Keep work out of your bedroom
- [ ] Say no to non-urgent meetings

**Remember:** Burnout often results from blurred boundaries between work and personal life.

### 3. Prioritize Personal Wellness

**Physical Self-Care:**
- [ ] Exercise regularly (even 30 min/day helps)
- [ ] Get consistent sleep (7-9 hours)
- [ ] Eat regular, healthy meals
- [ ] Limit caffeine and alcohol
- [ ] Take movement breaks every hour

**Mental Self-Care:**
- [ ] Practice meditation or mindfulness (use CLARO!)
- [ ] Journal your thoughts and feelings
- [ ] Practice deep breathing exercises
- [ ] Spend time in nature
- [ ] Engage in hobbies you enjoy

**Social Self-Care:**
- [ ] Maintain friendships outside work
- [ ] Connect with family regularly
- [ ] Participate in social activities
- [ ] Join groups with shared interests
- [ ] Build supportive relationships

### 4. Build Professional Efficacy

**Grow Your Skills:**
- [ ] Seek training opportunities
- [ ] Learn new skills to increase confidence
- [ ] Ask for mentorship
- [ ] Share your expertise with others
- [ ] Take on projects where you can succeed

**Regain Purpose:**
- [ ] Reconnect with why you do this work
- [ ] Focus on positive impact you make
- [ ] Set meaningful professional goals
- [ ] Celebrate small wins
- [ ] Help others succeed

### 5. Foster Supportive Connections

**Build Relationships:**
- [ ] Connect with colleagues who support you
- [ ] Find a mentor or peer buddy
- [ ] Join employee resource groups
- [ ] Participate in team activities
- [ ] Support coworkers facing challenges

**Create Psychological Safety:**
- [ ] Be honest about challenges (appropriately)
- [ ] Ask for help when needed
- [ ] Share concerns with your manager
- [ ] Speak up about unsustainable situations
- [ ] Normalize conversations about mental health

## What to Do If You're Struggling

### Step 1: Self-Awareness
✅ **Complete the Burnout Assessment** in CLARO
✅ **Track your symptoms** for a week
✅ **Identify key stressors** and triggers
✅ **Assess your current coping** strategies

### Step 2: Take Small Actions
✅ **Start meditation practice** - even 5 minutes daily
✅ **Improve sleep** - set a consistent schedule
✅ **Add exercise** - just 20-30 minutes helps
✅ **Connect with others** - reach out to a friend
✅ **Try journaling** - process your feelings

### Step 3: Talk to Your Manager
**What to Say:**
"I've been feeling overwhelmed lately, and I want to address it. Can we talk about my workload and stress levels? I'd like to work together on solutions."

**What Your Manager Can Help With:**
- Redistribute or reduce workload temporarily
- Offer flexible working arrangements
- Provide access to mental health resources
- Increase check-ins and support
- Adjust deadlines or expectations

### Step 4: Use Available Resources
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP)**: Counseling and support
- **Mental Health Services**: Access through HR
- **Meditation App (CLARO)**: Daily practice and guidance
- **Wellness Programs**: Exercise, nutrition, stress management
- **Support Groups**: Connect with others in similar situations

### Step 5: Seek Professional Help
**Consider speaking with a mental health professional if:**
- Symptoms persist despite self-care efforts
- You're having thoughts of harm
- Symptoms are interfering with daily functioning
- You've tried interventions without improvement
- You feel you need professional diagnosis/treatment

**Finding Help:**
- Ask HR for therapist referrals
- Contact your insurance for coverage
- Look into EAP services (usually free)
- Ask your doctor for recommendations

## Conversations to Have

### With Your Manager
"I want to ensure I'm doing sustainable work. Can we discuss:
- My current workload and priorities
- Realistic timelines for projects
- Support or resources I might need
- Flexible arrangements that could help
- My capacity for new responsibilities"

### With HR/EAP
"I'm feeling overwhelmed and would like support. Can you help me:
- Understand available mental health resources
- Explore EAP counseling services
- Discuss flexible work arrangements
- Learn about wellness programs
- Connect with occupational health if needed"

### With a Trusted Colleague
"I've been struggling lately and want to talk. Would you be open to:
- Listening and offering support
- Helping me brainstorm solutions
- Checking in with me regularly
- Connecting me with others who might help"

## Recovery & Resilience

### If You're in Crisis
- **Crisis hotline**: [1-800-273-8255]
- **Text line**: Text HOME to [741741]
- **Emergency**: Call 911 or go to nearest ER
- **EAP Crisis line**: Check your company benefits

### Short-term Recovery (Weeks 1-4)
1. Get professional support (therapy, counseling)
2. Establish basic self-care routine
3. Communicate with manager about support needs
4. Reduce work stress where possible
5. Connect with supportive people
6. Use meditation and wellness tools
7. Monitor sleep and nutrition

### Medium-term Recovery (Months 2-3)
1. Continue professional support
2. Build sustainable habits
3. Gradually increase activity
4. Reconnect with purpose
5. Rebuild confidence
6. Address relationship impacts
7. Plan return to normal functioning

### Long-term Prevention (Ongoing)
1. Maintain healthy boundaries
2. Sustain wellness practices
3. Stay connected to support network
4. Monitor warning signs
5. Continue professional support if beneficial
6. Build resilience through challenge
7. Help others who are struggling

## Build Resilience

### 10 Daily Habits for Burnout Prevention

1. **Meditation** (5-10 min daily)
2. **Exercise** (20-30 min, most days)
3. **Sleep** (7-9 hours consistently)
4. **Healthy eating** (nutritious meals)
5. **Breaks** (movement every hour)
6. **Gratitude** (appreciate 3 things daily)
7. **Connection** (talk to someone daily)
8. **Boundaries** (work hours end at [time])
9. **Hobbies** (do something you enjoy)
10. **Reflection** (journal or check-in)

### Resilience Mindset

**Instead of:**
- "I have to do it all"
- "I'm failing if I ask for help"
- "My worth is my productivity"
- "I can't say no"
- "Work comes first"

**Try:**
- "I'll do what's possible well"
- "Asking for help shows strength"
- "My worth is inherent and unchanging"
- "No is protecting my wellbeing"
- "My health comes first"
