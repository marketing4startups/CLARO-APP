## Part 1: Market Analysis & Positioning

### The Burnout Crisis

**The Problem:**
- **62% of employees** report high/moderate burnout globally
- **$15-30B annual cost** to US economy (healthcare, turnover, lost productivity)
- **1 in 3 workers** have already left jobs due to burnout
- **Median cost to replace** a burned-out employee: 150% of salary
- **Preventable**: 60% more effective when caught early

**Why Current Solutions Fail:**
- ❌ Generic wellness apps (meditation doesn't solve workload issues)
- ❌ EAP-only models (crisis-reactive, not preventive)
- ❌ Manager dashboards without tools (data without action)
- ❌ No validated assessment framework
- ❌ Siloed from daily work experience

**The CLARO Difference:**
- ✅ Validated burnout assessment (Maslach Burnout Inventory)
- ✅ Manager-specific tools and training
- ✅ Tiered intervention system (prevent → early → intensive → crisis)
- ✅ Integrated prevention strategies
- ✅ Real organizational culture transformation

### Target Markets

#### Primary: Mid-Market HR Leaders (250-2,000 employees)
- **Pain Points:**
  - High voluntary turnover (tech, finance, healthcare)
  - Managers lacking training on mental health
  - Expensive EAP programs with low utilization
  - Difficulty demonstrating mental health ROI
  - Competitive talent market requiring retention focus
  
- **Buying Triggers:**
  - New HR technology investment cycle
  - CEO/Board mandate on employee wellness
  - Retention crisis after layoffs
  - Mental health benefits audit
  - Merger integration requiring culture alignment

- **Decision Makers:**
  - Chief People Officer / VP HR
  - Employee Experience Leader
  - Organizational Development Manager
  - Benefits/Wellness Director
  - CEO (wellness initiative champion)

- **Budget Authority:**
  - HR Technology budget: $5K-50K annually
  - Wellness budget increase: +15-20% post-pandemic
  - Approval authority: C-level or Board

#### Secondary: Enterprise (2,000+ employees)
- **Differentiation:**
  - Multi-location deployment
  - Advanced analytics and reporting
  - Custom integrations (Workday, SuccessFactors, etc.)
  - Dedicated support and success management
  - Executive dashboards for CEO/Board reporting

#### Tertiary: Managed HR Services
- **HR Consulting Firms** (ADP, Mercer, Willis Towers Watson)
- **EAP Providers** (Magellan, Beacon Health, Spring Health)
- **Benefits Brokers** (need prevention layer)
- **Insurance Companies** (interested in cost reduction)
