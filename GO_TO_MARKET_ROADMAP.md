## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Slow Sales Cycle
**Mitigation:**
- Qualify leads aggressively early
- Use free trial to accelerate evaluation
- Executive involvement for larger deals
- Focus on partner channel for faster adoption

### Risk 2: Market Education Needed
**Mitigation:**
- Aggressive thought leadership campaign
- Webinar series (education-focused)
- Speaking at industry conferences
- Content marketing focus on awareness

### Risk 3: Sales Team Ramp
**Mitigation:**
- Hire experienced VP Sales (not first-time)
- Structured onboarding and training
- Weekly coaching and feedback
- Clear activity metrics and expectations

### Risk 4: Competition
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on manager-powered angle (differentiated)
- Build strong partnerships
- Customer success excellence
- Continuous product innovation
