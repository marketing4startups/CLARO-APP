## Part 8: FAQ Document

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How is CLARO different from our existing wellness program?**

A: CLARO is burnout-prevention specific, while wellness programs are general. CLARO gives managers tools and visibility; wellness programs are usually employee-focused. You can use both together—in fact, they're complementary.

---

**Q: Will employees feel surveilled?**

A: No. Assessments are voluntary, employees control their data, and the platform is transparent. Managers see group insights, not individual tracking. It's about support, not surveillance.

---

**Q: How long does implementation take?**

A: Typically 2-3 weeks from contract to live:
- Week 1: Deploy, configure, manager training
- Week 2: Employee communication, baseline assessment
- Week 3: Monitoring and first interventions

---

**Q: What's the ROI?**

A: Average ROI is 27x in Year 1 (payback in 2-3 months). For a 500-person organization, typically $750K-1.5M in first-year savings through prevented resignations alone.

---

**Q: Can you integrate with our HR system?**

A: Yes. We support:
- SSO (SAML, OAuth)
- Directory sync (AD, Okta)
- HRIS integrations (Workday, ADP, SuccessFactors)
- Custom API integrations available

---

**Q: What about data security and privacy?**

A: SOC 2 Type II certified, HIPAA and GDPR compliant, data encrypted at rest and in transit. Employees own their data and can delete anytime.
